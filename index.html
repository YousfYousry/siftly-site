GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity.

Does Siftly delete my photos automatically?+

You decide what to move to Trash. Permanent deletion requires a separate confirmation. Undo and Restore help you recover files before permanent deletion.

What can I do for free?+

Free includes manual swipe review, photos, videos and GIFs, Favorites, Trash, Undo, Restore, basic local compression and duplicate previews. Pro adds tools such as similar-picture detection, unlimited duplicate cleanup, custom folders and advanced compression. Prices and subscription terms are shown in the app.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?+

Siftly does not upload your photos or videos. Media processing happens on your device. The app uses advertising, analytics and subscription services; see the privacy policy for their data practices.

Can I get it on iPhone and Android?+

Use the download page to choose your platform. Store links will be added here when confirmed. Availability may vary by platform and region.
